PRINCIPLES
01
The picture is the hero.
Panels are cards around the stage; their chrome stays quiet so colour on the stage is the only loud thing.
02
One accent, always meaning "here".
Selection, focus, the playhead and the primary action share it; nothing else does.
03
Touch it, don't type it.
Scrub numbers, drag curves, click a colour to pick it; the keyboard has Ctrl K for everything else.
04
Always say what is happening.
Saved, rendering, stale, cached: shown where you are looking, never only on a page you left.
