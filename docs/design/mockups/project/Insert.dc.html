Groups. Select and Hand | the insert tools | Anchor, split by hairlines.
Insert tools show the glyph of the variant used last and a small corner triangle. A click arms the tool (the next click on the stage places the variant); the triangle, a right-click or a long press opens the flyout, with the keys at the right and the last-used variant marked in the accent.
Keys (canvas focused): V H P, R L T E I X D; O, Shift+R, Shift+L ... pick a variant at once. Esc goes back to Select.
